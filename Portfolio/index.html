About

A Deep Dive into How I Build

I'm Shayan, an AI MERN Stack Engineer who ships full, working products — not tutorial clones. Every project I take on starts as a real problem for a real user, and ends as something deployed, tested, and actually usable.

My work sits at the intersection of the MERN stack and practical AI: chat concierges that answer real questions, scoring engines that grade real answers, and analytics pipelines that surface real insight from MongoDB aggregation. I care as much about the auth flow and the checkout as I do about the AI feature on top.

I'm currently open for freelance and contract work — if you have a product that needs a full build or an existing site that needs an AI layer, I'd like to hear about it.
